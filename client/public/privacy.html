Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

Zinda Play (including Simply) is operated by Neo jobzinda private limited. This policy describes how we handle information through our website and mobile app. Contact: jobzinda@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile: username, password hash, account type, approval status, optional display name, bio, business details and introduction video. Creator applications require a profile photo; introduction videos are optional. If you enable account recovery, we store your verified recovery email, pending email changes, hashed verification/reset tokens and temporary request-rate counters. New accounts provide an email address at signup; it is saved with the account, a verification code is sent to it, and it is visible to Zinda Play administrators for account management. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, not plain text.
  • Content and activity: uploaded videos, thumbnails, captions, comments, likes, follows, saved videos, reports, invitations and messages; referral attribution, watch events, ad impressions/clicks and estimated earnings.
  • Payments: selected membership/ad plans, payment screenshots, transaction and review records, and withdrawal/payment details you provide. Manual payment verification is handled by our administrators. Never upload a PIN, password or full card credentials in payment evidence.
  • Technical information: IP addresses available to our servers/hosting providers, IP-derived approximate location where configured, hashed IP-based view identifiers, request/error logs and login sessions. We do not request precise GPS location for city targeting.

How we use information

We use this information to run accounts and video playback; review uploads and membership payments; provide comments, messages, referrals and notifications; deliver and measure advertising; estimate creator/promoter earnings; prevent duplicate rewards, abuse and fraud; troubleshoot problems and meet applicable obligations. Selecting files for upload gives us access to the files you select, not your entire photo library.

Public information and sharing

Approved published videos, creator/business public profiles, public comments and activity counts may be visible to other users and visitors. Shared video links may expose the video's title, description and thumbnail to messaging/social services that generate previews. Do not upload information you do not want published. Private messages and payment evidence are not intended to be public; administrators can access records needed for support, moderation and verification.

Service providers and advertising

We use hosting and database services (including Vercel, Render and MongoDB) and Bunny Stream/CDN for video storage/delivery. Brevo processes recovery email addresses and transactional messages to deliver email verification, password-reset links and username reminders. These providers process information needed to deliver the service and may operate in other countries. MaxMind-based IP geolocation may be used for approximate city targeting. Ads can use content interests and approximate location; advertisers receive campaign performance information. Opening advertiser or other external links is subject to the destination's own privacy policy. We do not sell account information.

Browser/app storage and notifications

We use browser session/local storage and mobile secure storage for login, referral attribution and preferences such as content interests. Clearing storage or signing out can remove local settings. In-app notifications are available. Device push notifications and sound are not currently enabled; if introduced, device permissions and this policy will be updated as appropriate.

Retention and account deletion

You can delete your account in the web menu or mobile Profile menu using Delete account, your current password and the confirmation word DELETE. See account deletion instructions. This removes the account/profile, uploaded media and associated social content, stops linked ads and revokes login sessions. Deletion is irreversible. If a storage provider is unavailable, content is disabled and the app shows an error; retry deletion to finish.

Limited payment evidence, transaction/earnings/withdrawal records and non-public deleted-video/ad ledger references may remain where needed for reconciliation, outstanding payments, fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations. Deleting an account does not automatically pay out balances or refund purchases; contact us about outstanding amounts before deleting. We have not published a fixed financial-record retention period; contact us for details and requests. Provider backups, logs and previously cached/shared copies may persist under their retention practices and are not necessarily removed immediately. We cannot erase copies independently saved by others.

Your choices and requests

You may edit your profile, remove your videos, sign out, delete your account or contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. If you cannot access your account, email jobzinda@gmail.com; we may verify ownership before acting. Do not send your password. Rights and any exceptions depend on applicable law.

Security and children

We use measures including password hashing, authenticated access and HTTPS on our public services. No system is completely secure. The service is not directed to children who cannot lawfully consent to its use; a parent/guardian can contact us about a child's information for review and removal as appropriate.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service changes, with a revised effective date. For privacy concerns contact Neo jobzinda private limited at jobzinda@gmail.com.
