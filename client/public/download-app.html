How to install

  1. Tap Download Android APK above and wait for the download to finish.
  2. Open zinda-play.apk from your browser downloads or your phone's Files app.
  3. If Android asks, open Settings and allow Install unknown apps for the browser or Files app you used. Wording varies by phone.
  4. Return to the APK, tap Install, then Open. You can turn off the installation permission afterwards.

Only install an APK you trust from the official Zinda Play website. Keep Google Play Protect enabled. If Android reports that an update conflicts with an installed app, do not uninstall it just to bypass the error; contact support first to avoid losing local data.

The website starts the download, but Android requires you to approve installation. Some managed devices do not permit APK installation.
